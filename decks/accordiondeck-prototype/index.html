Five variants of the native details element, for #361. Each section says what to listen for. Variant headings below are h2, so they do not mix with the h3 headings inside the summaries.

Variant 1: heading inside the summary

Listen for: is each "Shipping", "Returns", "Warranty" announced as a heading (and in the headings list), and is it still announced as expandable and as expanded or collapsed?

Shipping

We ship in two days.

Returns

Return within thirty days.

Warranty

Two years of cover.

Variant 2: plain summary

Listen for: the baseline. Is "Shipping" announced as expandable, and is expanded or collapsed announced on toggle? No heading is expected.

Shipping
We ship in two days.
Returns
Return within thirty days.
Warranty
Two years of cover.

Variant 3: heading plus aria-expanded on the summary

Listen for: the same as variant 1. Then: does adding aria-expanded make iOS VoiceOver announce expanded and collapsed where variant 1 stayed silent? Is anything announced twice?

Shipping

We ship in two days.

Returns

Return within thirty days.

Warranty

Two years of cover.

Variant 4: named group of five

Use find-in-page (Safari: Find on Page) for "marmalade", "quokka", "zephyr", "tamarind", "obsidian". Item 1 is open. Listen for and watch: does the match open its item, close the open one, and scroll to the match? Also try the fragment links below.
